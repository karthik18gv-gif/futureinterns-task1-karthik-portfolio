About

Background & goals

I'm a final-year MCA student at Community Institute of Management Studies (CIMS), Bangalore City University, maintaining a CGPA of 8.71. Before this, I completed my BCA at NRI Institute, Bangalore, and worked as a Web Development Intern at Vinqube Technologies, where I got my first real taste of shipping code that other people depend on.

What I actually enjoy is building systems that model real processes — citizen services, pet adoption, hostel management, interview scheduling — rather than tutorial-style CRUD apps. I like the backend as much as the interface: designing the database, getting the business logic right, and then making sure the UI doesn't get in the way.

I'm currently focused on entering the IT industry as a Software Engineer / Full-Stack Developer, with a growing interest in cloud computing (AWS) — I run my cloud experiments out of the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region.
